# Coreves Software Solutions — Business Identity & Foundations

Core document for strategic direction, positioning, and operational guardrails.

## 1. Mission & Vision

**Mission — what we do today**
To build reliable, purpose-built software that empowers microfinance and lending institutions in Africa to manage credit operations with speed, accuracy, and confidence.

**Vision — what we're building toward**
To become the leading provider of financial technology infrastructure for emerging-market lenders — making credit accessible, transparent, and efficiently managed across Africa.

## 2. Core Identity — Who We Are

**What we are**
A software product company, not a generic IT services shop. We build opinionated, focused tools for the financial services sector.

**Who we serve**
Microfinance institutions, SACCOs, and lending businesses — primarily in Zambia and the wider Southern and East Africa region.

**How we show up**
Trustworthy, technically rigorous, and client-close. We understand the lending context, not just the code.

**Our competitive edge**
Local market knowledge paired with professional-grade software delivery. Built for African lending realities — not adapted from elsewhere.

## 3. Core Values

- **Reliability** — The systems our clients depend on must work — every time. Stability is not optional in financial software.
- **Transparency** — In pricing, in delivery timelines, in what our software does. No surprises for clients.
- **Purposeful innovation** — We build what solves real problems for our market — not features for their own sake.
- **Quality execution** — Every deliverable — code, document, or client call — reflects the standard we set for ourselves.

## 4. Product & Market Focus

**Flagship product**
Loan Management System (LMS) for microfinance and lending institutions

**Primary market**
Zambia — expanding to Southern and East Africa

**Target customers**
- Microfinance institutions (MFIs)
- SACCOs and credit unions
- Private lending companies
- Digital lenders and fintech startups

**Core problem solved**
Replacing manual, error-prone loan tracking with automated, auditable, and compliant credit management software.

**Product phase**
Active development — first commercial version targeting Zambian lenders.

**Adjacent opportunities**
- Mobile money integrations (MTN Zambia, Airtel Zambia)
- Credit scoring modules
- Regulatory reporting tools

## 5. Standards of Execution — The Guardrails

- **Security is non-negotiable** — Every product handles financial data. Security audits, proper authentication, and data protection are built in — not bolted on after the fact.
- **We don't ship what we can't support** — Only take on clients and features we can deliver to a professional standard. Scope clarity must be established before contracts are signed.
- **Documentation is part of the product** — User guides, API docs, and onboarding materials ship alongside the software — not as an afterthought.
- **Pricing reflects value, not desperation** — Quote professionally. Don't undercut to win clients who aren't the right fit. Protect the business model.
- **Client feedback drives the roadmap** — New features are validated against real lending workflows — not assumed from the outside.
- **Compliance is a feature** — Our LMS must make it easier for clients to meet Bank of Zambia and regulatory requirements — not harder.
